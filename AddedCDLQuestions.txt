Here are 25 CDL knowledge-base questions and answers written as standalone entries for study or database use.
#	Question	Answer
1	What is the purpose of a commercial vehicle pre-trip inspection?	To identify defects or safety problems before operating the vehicle.
2	What does maintaining three points of contact mean when entering or exiting a truck?	Keep two hands and one foot, or two feet and one hand, in contact with the vehicle.
3	Which fluid levels should a driver check during an engine-compartment inspection?	Engine oil, coolant, power-steering fluid, and windshield-washer fluid; check other fluids as applicable to the vehicle.
4	Why should a driver avoid removing a radiator cap while the engine is hot?	Pressurized hot coolant and steam can escape and cause severe burns.
5	Which tire defects require correction before driving a commercial vehicle?	Exposed cords or belts, tread or sidewall separation, a flat tire, an audible leak, or a cut exposing ply or belt material.
6	What is the minimum tread depth for the front tires of a commercial truck or tractor?	At least 4/32 inch in a major tread groove.
7	What is the minimum tread depth for commercial vehicle tires other than the front tires?	Generally, at least 2/32 inch in a major tread groove.
8	Why should a driver avoid releasing air from hot tires to reduce pressure?	Tire pressure normally rises as tires heat up. Releasing air can leave them underinflated after they cool.
9	What can rust streaks around a wheel’s lug nuts indicate?	They can indicate loose lug nuts and require further inspection.
10	Why must cargo be properly secured in a commercial vehicle?	Unsecured cargo can shift, fall, affect steering and stability, or cause a crash.
11	What is a vehicle’s blind spot?	An area around the vehicle that the driver cannot see directly or through properly adjusted mirrors.
12	Why should a commercial driver check mirrors regularly?	To monitor surrounding traffic, vehicle position, tires, and visible cargo.
13	What is the safest way to back a commercial vehicle when a helper is available?	Agree on signals, keep the helper in sight, and stop immediately if visual contact is lost.
14	Why should a driver inspect the area behind a vehicle before backing?	To identify people, obstacles, overhead hazards, and clearance problems that may be difficult to see from the cab.
15	What are the three main parts of total stopping distance?	Perception distance, reaction distance, and braking distance.
16	How does increased speed affect braking distance?	Braking distance increases sharply; under similar conditions, doubling speed can make braking distance approximately four times longer.
17	When should a commercial driver increase following distance?	When visibility is poor, roads are slippery, traffic conditions are hazardous, or additional stopping space is needed.
18	What is hydroplaning?	A condition in which tires ride on water and lose contact with the road, reducing steering and braking control.
19	What should a driver do if the vehicle begins hydroplaning?	Ease off the accelerator, avoid sudden braking or steering, and allow the vehicle to slow until traction returns.
20	Why should a driver select the proper gear before starting down a steep downgrade?	The correct gear helps engine braking control speed and reduces reliance on the service brakes.
21	What is brake fade?	A reduction in braking effectiveness caused by excessive heat, often from prolonged or improper brake use.
22	Why is a high center of gravity dangerous in a commercial vehicle?	It increases the risk of rollover, especially during curves, sudden turns, or abrupt maneuvers.
23	What should a driver do when becoming sleepy while driving?	Stop at a safe location and rest. Caffeine, open windows, and loud music cannot replace sleep.
24	What should a driver do first if the commercial vehicle catches fire while moving?	Pull off the road safely and stop in an open area away from people, buildings, and combustible materials. Notify emergency services.
25	Where should a driver aim a fire extinguisher when fighting a small vehicle fire?	Aim at the base of the fire from a safe distance, staying upwind and keeping an escape route available.

26	What is the purpose of an air compressor in an air-brake system?	It supplies compressed air to the storage tanks for brake operation.
27	What does the air-compressor governor control?	It controls when the compressor pumps air into the tanks and when it stops pumping.
28	Why must air-brake storage tanks be drained?	To remove accumulated water and oil that can damage components or freeze in cold weather.

31	What holds spring brakes released during normal driving?	Compressed air holds powerful mechanical springs back.
32	What is air-brake lag?	The delay between pressing the brake pedal and air pressure reaching the brakes to apply them.
33	Why should a driver avoid repeatedly pumping air brakes unnecessarily?	Each application uses stored air and can reduce pressure faster than the compressor replaces it.
34	What is the purpose of an antilock braking system, or ABS?	It helps prevent wheel lockup during braking so the driver can maintain steering control.
35	How should a driver brake during an emergency in a vehicle equipped with ABS?	Apply firm, controlled brake pressure without pumping the pedal; ABS automatically adjusts pressure to help prevent wheel lockup.
36	What is offtracking in a combination vehicle?	The rear wheels follow a path inside the path taken by the front wheels during a turn.
37	What is trailer rearward amplification?	A sudden steering movement can produce increasingly strong sideways movement in the rear trailers, especially in doubles and triples.
38	What is a tractor-trailer jackknife?	A condition in which the tractor and trailer fold toward each other at a sharp angle, usually after a loss of traction.
39	What is the purpose of the fifth wheel on a tractor?	It couples the semitrailer to the tractor and supports part of the trailer’s weight.
40	What is the kingpin on a semitrailer?	The steel pin that engages the tractor’s fifth-wheel locking mechanism.
41	Where should fifth-wheel locking jaws close during proper coupling?	Around the shank of the kingpin, below its enlarged head.
42	What is a fifth-wheel coupling tug test?	With the trailer brakes applied, the driver gently pulls forward to check that the coupling holds. A visual inspection is also required.
43	What are glad hands on a tractor-trailer?	Coupling devices that connect the tractor’s and trailer’s air lines.
44	What does the trailer service air line do?	It carries air-pressure signals that control the trailer’s service brakes.
45	What does the trailer emergency or supply air line do?	It supplies air to the trailer’s tanks and controls emergency-brake operation when supply pressure is lost.
