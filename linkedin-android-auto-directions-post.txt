A voice-assisted location lookup with a handoff to Google Maps.

I added a Google Maps handoff to my local knowledge assistant. Ask for the nearest supported restaurant or travel stop, review the route preview, then tap “Navigate in Google Maps.”

To use it from a phone, add the web app to the home screen using the browser’s “Add to Home Screen” option. Open the shortcut, allow location access, and tap the navigation link for the selected destination. This opens the regular Google Maps app on the phone. Tap Start in Maps for spoken turn-by-turn guidance.

This is a Google Maps handoff, not native Android Auto integration. If the phone is connected to Android Auto, Maps may appear there through its normal connected-phone behavior, but the web app does not control that display or navigation. The in-app “Close Directions” button closes the route preview; stop active navigation in Google Maps.

It’s a small integration, but it makes a useful bridge between asking a question by voice and getting the route onto the screen drivers already use.
